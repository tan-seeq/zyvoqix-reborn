# ZYVOQIX game sources & legal notes

- ZYVOQIX is a discovery hub. Games from Yandex, CrazyGames, Poki and other portals remain on their original websites. Free-to-play does not mean we can copy their executables or screenshots.
- 142 community links use [sausi-7/games](https://github.com/sausi-7/games), MIT License, Copyright (c) 2026 Saurabh Singh. The embedded iframe loads the upstream original; no copies are hosted in ZYVOQIX. Game and trademark assets may carry additional rights.
- Commercial third-party games can be embedded after publisher approval through a distributor such as GameDistribution. Until approved, use outbound links only.
- Data source: public pages and public repositories, compiled 2026-10-20. Links may change; check before promoting.
- Catalog stored in /public/library.json. Editing, committing to GitHub and automated Render deploys refresh the library.
