# Third-party notices

Old San Juan Kart itself is MIT licensed — see [LICENSE](LICENSE).

The game ships **no binary assets**. There are no image, audio, video, model or
font files anywhere in this repository. Every texture, sound, piece of music and
3D model is generated by code at runtime, from primitives (`CanvasRenderingContext2D`
for textures, the WebAudio graph for sound, and Three.js geometry for models).
That is a deliberate design constraint, and it is what keeps this project free of
third-party asset licensing.

The list below therefore covers only *software* dependencies and the two web
fonts the page links to.

## Emoji

Item icons in the HUD are Unicode codepoints (`U+1F965` coconut, `U+1F367`
shaved ice, and so on), rendered by whatever emoji font the player's operating
system provides. No emoji artwork is included in or distributed by this project.

## Content and likenesses

- **Landmark geometry** — El Morro, San Cristóbal, the Capitolio, the Coliseo,
  the Hato Rey towers and so on are original low-poly interpretations, modelled
  from scratch in code. In the United States, of which Puerto Rico is a
  jurisdiction, 17 U.S.C. § 120(a) expressly permits pictorial representations
  of architectural works that are ordinarily visible from a public place. The
  Castillos San Felipe del Morro and San Cristóbal are National Park Service
  sites; works of the U.S. federal government carry no copyright.
- **Road geography** — the Expreso Las Américas course follows a real corridor.
  The layout of a public road is a geographic fact and is not copyrightable. The
  control points were authored by hand from written driving directions; **no map
  data, tiles or extracts were used**, so no map database licence (OpenStreetMap's
  ODbL or otherwise) applies.
- **Signage** — road signs carry real place names and route numbers, which are
  government designations used descriptively. Building signage uses generic
  Spanish common nouns (`PLAZA`, `COLISEO`, `ESTADIO`). No company logo, wordmark
  or trade dress is reproduced anywhere in the game.
- **The flag of Puerto Rico** is an official government symbol in use since 1895
  and is not subject to copyright.
- **Music** is generated at runtime from a traditional Afro-Cuban 3-2 son clave
  and a ii-V-I-VI chord turnaround. Rhythmic patterns of traditional folk music
  and harmonic progressions are not copyrightable subject matter, and no melody
  from any existing composition is reproduced or transcribed.
- **Characters and items** are original designs drawn from Puerto Rican folk
  culture (the coquí, the vejigante, the piragüero). Game mechanics are not
  copyrightable; no character, name, mark or artwork from any other game is used.
