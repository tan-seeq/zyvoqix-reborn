ABOUT ZYVOQIX

A new universe of browser games

A gaming website and interactive school ICT project featuring original HTML5 games.

How to play

Choose Play Now, select a game and press Start. Use arrows or WASD to move and Space for action, or use touch controls on your phone.

Privacy & saved progress

Game records, favorites and nicknames are stored only on your device. We do not collect email addresses. External game links follow their providers' rules.

Fair play & game rights

Original Zyvoqix mini-games run here. Third-party games remain on their own official websites; links do not imply sponsorship or affiliation.

The ICT project

Built with HTML, CSS, JavaScript, WebGL and Canvas. Responsive layouts, event handling, game loops and browser storage demonstrate practical web-development skills.
